EMBERLINE — Halcyon Works: an original round-based tactical FPS on three.js, built for make.fun's threejs-fps-01 bounty. Wardens hold a desert solar refinery against Ember bots who plant a charge at site A or B; buy-phase economy, five weapons plus a frag grenade, headshot gunplay with recoil and spread, waypoint-driven bot tactics, a heading compass, and drifting dusk dust over the yard.
